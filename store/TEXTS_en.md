## Full description
Kyv kytsh ("Word Circle") is a word puzzle in the Komi (Komi-Zyrian) language.

Swipe across the letters on the wheel to form Komi words and fill the crossword. Special letters Ӧ and І are right on the wheel — no typing needed.

• 500 levels in 50 chapters: meadow, forest, taiga (parma), the Vychegda, Izhma and Pechora rivers, reindeer, folk tales, ornaments… from easy words to hard ones.
• 50 beautiful northern landscapes — one for each chapter.
• A card for every word you find: Russian translation, meanings and examples.
• "My dictionary" with all the words you have learned.
• Daily puzzle, word of the day, daily reward, quests and a wheel of fortune.
• Bonus words and hints: reveal a letter, reveal a word, shuffle.
• Interactive tutorial on first launch. Works offline.

The interface is in Russian; translations are into Russian.

Dictionary data: GiellaLT / Giellatekno Komi–Russian dictionary (CC BY 4.0), Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0).
